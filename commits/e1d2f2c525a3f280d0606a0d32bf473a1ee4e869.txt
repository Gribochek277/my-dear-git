Rebuild the TUI shell to the GUI-parity layout
Mirror AppLayout.tsx in monochrome: a repo bar, a framed sidebar with a
draggable divider, a right column with a toolbar row, Changes|History tabs
and the framed content, and a one-row truncated status bar.

- layout.ts owns the whole geometry: it resolves ui-store pixel sizes into
  cells that tile the terminal exactly (sidebar >= 16, right column >= 24,
  commit panel >= 3 rows), exposes absolute pane/divider rectangles and the
  keyboard + drag resize math with the same clamps.
- App.tsx composes the shell from those numbers, cycles Tab/Shift+Tab over
  the physical panes (files/diff/commit or list/detail plus sidebar and
  status), and hands the exact content rectangle to the views.
- StatusView and HistoryView render their columns as titled Panels with the
  commit panel pinned under the file list and LayoutDividers between the
  panes; LayoutDivider wires a gesture to the ui-store (live update, release
  commits, Esc restores).
- RepoBar and a placeholder Toolbar are new; the status bar gains
  ahead/behind and a busy spinner; Panel gained a clip opt-out for the
  sidebar's full-screen dialogs.
- Removes the obsolete BorderHandles/border-resize/shell-focus modules and
  updates docs/tui-architecture.md with the parity layout.
