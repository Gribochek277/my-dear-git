Rebuild the TUI history view to the GUI-parity layout
The history pane now matches the GUI history view cell for cell:

- the commit-list column keeps its framed `History (n)` panel and gains the
  GUI filter bar (`All branches` / `Current branch`, active bold+inverse,
  inactive dim) with the refresh action and the checked-out branch;
- the working tree's `Uncommitted changes` row sits above the graph while the
  tree is dirty and is the cursor's top position (`k` from the newest commit);
  selecting it shows the GUI's `Switch to Changes view to see uncommitted
  diffs` hint, and the no-selection state shows `Select a commit to view
  changed files`;
- the paging footer mirrors the GUI: a clickable `Load more…` button
  alongside the near-the-end auto-load, a spinner while a page is in flight
  and the `end of history` marker at the bottom;
- the detail panel gains the commit meta block (subject over
  `hash · author · date`) above the changed files and the diff, and keeps the
  cherry-pick action and the file-history overlay;
- the file-history, n/p, Ctrl+d/u and reset bindings are inert on the
  uncommitted row, where there is no commit to act on.

Tests: a new `history-parity.test.tsx` covers the filter bar labels and
switching, row content (graph, refs, hash, author, date), the detail meta
block with files and diff, the uncommitted row (keyboard and click), the
empty state, the load-more button with the end marker and wheel scrolling;
`history-layout`/`history-format` cover the new pure helpers.
