Give the TUI status bar, repo bar and welcome the GUI chrome
Mirror the GUI's StatusBar/RepoTabs/WelcomeScreen trio in the parity shell:

- StatusBar is one truncated line on a fixed row: `repo-name | path` on the
  left, then branch, ahead/behind, the focused pane, the busy spinner,
  `mouse: on/off` and a clickable `[s] Settings` cell pinned to the right
  edge. statusBarColumns() owns the widths (settings first, then the state
  cluster, then the repo line, then the hint strip capped at 48 cells), so a
  narrow terminal truncates the segments with `…` instead of wrapping and the
  settings affordance never disappears.
- RepoBar gains the close affordance next to the switcher hint: `[x] close`
  is a mouse region and `Ctrl+W` closes the active repository (back to the
  welcome state or the next open one); the row still opens the switcher on
  click. The welcome state keeps showing the app title instead.
- Welcome renders the GUI's action row on the shell's welcome card: `[o] Open
  Repository` (the shim's open-repo dialog), `[c] Clone Repository` and `[s]
  Settings`, each a mouse region with its key. The repo switcher reuses the
  bare list, unchanged.
- DialogsLayer's DIALOG_KEYS gain `s` (settings, global) and `c` (clone,
  welcome state only, where no pane owns the key) and per-key gates, so the
  visible affordances and the keyboard reach the same dialogs.

Tests (node env, temp HOME + temp repo) cover the status bar content, the
column budget, single-line truncation at 48/80/200 columns, the repo bar on
and off, close by click and Ctrl+W, recent-repo navigation by key and double
click, the welcome entries opening clone/settings/open-by-path, and the
settings round-trip through the shim to the config file.
