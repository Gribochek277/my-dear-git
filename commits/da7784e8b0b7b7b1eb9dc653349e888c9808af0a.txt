its diff, so the border-handle tests' frame-wide `not.toContain('│')` /
`not.toContain('─')` assertions were no longer valid. Every indicator check

instead of searching the whole frame:

- appearance: the handle column is exactly ['│', '│'] while the gesture is
  active, the commit handle row is exactly a run of '─';
- disappearance: the same cells equal their pre-gesture values after the
  release, and the sidebar handle's original column is unpainted after Esc
  while the handle had followed the border and painted its new column
  mid-drag;
- tracking off: the attempted drag leaves the handle cells byte-identical.

Mutation-checked: removing the painted bar makes the appearance assertion
fail, and painting it unconditionally makes the cleared assertion fail, so
the tests still prove both directions.
