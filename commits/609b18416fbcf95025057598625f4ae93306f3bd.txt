Add the TUI monochrome kit: tone map, framed Panel, draggable divider
The TUI is monochrome by default from here on. src/tui/theme.ts owns the
semantic tone roles: monochrome maps them to ink attributes (bold, dim,
underline, inverse) and glyphs, and only --color / MYDEARGIT_TUI_COLOR=1
switches them to ANSI colours. Panel draws a titled box-drawing frame that
fills its rect exactly (long titles truncate with an ellipsis, children clip
inside the frame, focus is a bold frame against a dim unfocused one), and
ResizeDivider is the one-cell draggable line with an inverse affordance while
dragging and Esc-to-cancel.

Every TUI view is swept off direct colour props onto tone(): diff rows carry
the +/- sign with bold/dim, graph lanes draw bold nodes against dim lanes and
badges use one attribute per status. The text-entry placeholder is drawn in
repo as well, so ink-text-input's grey placeholder code never reaches a frame.
