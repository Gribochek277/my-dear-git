Keep nested dialogs' shields and rows from leaking clicks
Review findings: two hosts are mounted at once for nested flows (settings →
theme export/import, clone → Browse…), and a covered dialog keeps rendering
above the covering shield.

- DialogHost names its click shield per instance
  (`DIALOG_SHIELD_REGION_PREFIX` + `useId()`). The shared `dialog-shield` id let
  the inner host's unmount unregister the outer host's shield, after which
  clicks and wheel events fell through to the panes behind an open dialog.
- ToggleRow/ChoiceRow/InlineChoice take `enabled`; FormDialog passes its
  `interactive` state and also disables its own buttons while inactive, and the
  auxiliary buttons (clone `Browse…`, settings `Export theme…`/`Import theme…`)
  follow. A covered dialog's rows sit above the covering shield, so without
  this a click mutated its form values or pushed another dialog.
- Confirm gets the same treatment (buttons enabled only while active) and
  per-instance button region ids, so stacked confirmations cannot unregister
  each other's buttons.

Tests: clicks behind a dialog after a nested dialog/prompt closed (settings and
clone), clicks on a covered dialog's toggle and buttons, and an inactive
`Confirm`; all three fail against the pre-fix code. `helpers/mouse.tsx` gained
`BackgroundPane` and `waitForRegionMatching`, and the shared-primitives test
matches the shield id by prefix.

Also stabilised the history-view branch-filter tests found flaky in the full
suite: which rows are visible depends on commit dates, so they assert on the
loaded page and wait for measured regions.
