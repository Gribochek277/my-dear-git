Add shell mouse integration: border drag-resize and wheel routing
Completes the shell-level mouse behaviours on top of the app-shell's
MouseProvider and layout.ts, without touching the frozen App.tsx:

- BorderHandles (rendered by cli.tsx through App's `decorations` prop) puts
  one-cell drag handles on the sidebar|main and main|commit borders. Press
  starts the gesture, motion writes useUiStore.layout live with the same
  clamps as Ctrl+h/j/k/l, release persists it and Esc restores the layout
  captured at press. The handle paints the border line in reverse video
  while the drag is active and an input claim keeps the shell's global Esc
  cascade inert until it ends.
- border-resize.ts owns the handle rectangles and the pure drag -> layout
  patch math; mouse-contract.ts is the single written authority for the
  click/double-click/wheel/drag rules and the new wheelDeltaOf() helper.
- MouseRegistry routes a gesture down the region stack (hitTestChain):
  wheel-only regions let clicks fall through to the pane so the first click
  focuses it, rows without onWheel let the wheel reach the list below, and
  overlay shields keep capturing both. Sidebar's list wheel region and the
  history list now stay active while unfocused, so the wheel scrolls the
  pane under the pointer without changing focus. Horizontal wheel notches
  are ignored everywhere instead of being read as a scroll down.
- History pane click regions (rows, file rows, toolbar buttons) now follow
  the same two-step focus rule as the sidebar and the commit panel.
- Tests: pure border geometry/drag/clamp and wheelDeltaOf unit tests,
  registry chain-routing tests, and integration tests driving synthetic SGR
  press/motion/release and wheel sequences through the real shell (clamping
  at the minimums, Esc cancel, drag visual, non-TTY degradation, background
  pane scrolling, overlay wheel capture, horizontal wheel ignored).
