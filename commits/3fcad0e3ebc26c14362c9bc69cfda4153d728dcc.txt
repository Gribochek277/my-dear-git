Make TUI temp-dir cleanup non-fatal
A git child process can still hold files after a test finishes, and rmSync's 10x50ms retry budget was not always enough under parallel suites (ENOTEMPTY failed a merge check). Cleanup now retries for up to ~5s and swallows the final error: a leftover temp dir must never fail the suite.
