Wait for measured mouse regions in the dialog tests
`useMouseRegion` fills a region's rectangle in a post-layout effect, which can
lag `settle()` when the whole suite runs under load; aiming a synthetic report
at an unmeasured 0x0 rectangle silently misses. The shared
`tests/unit/tui/helpers/mouse.ts` helper waits for the measurement and clicks
the region's centre, replacing the settle-then-guess cells in the form, path,
clone and settings dialog tests (the same rule main's mouse-context tests use).
