Fix tag checkout hijacked by the remote-branch DWIM
`GitService.checkout` treated any `name/with/slash` as `<remote>/<branch>`
before plain resolution, so checking out a tag such as `release/v1.0` tried a
`v1.0` branch (silently switching to it when one existed) or failed on the
`-b v1.0 --track release/v1.0` fallback. The sidebar's tag row, its detached
HEAD warning and its success toast could therefore lie, and the GUI's tag
checkout had the same bug.

The service now resolves the exact ref before the heuristic: an existing local
branch of that full name wins (as in git), a tag is checked out with
`checkout --detach refs/tags/<name>` (never through the DWIM, so it cannot
become a `tags/<name>` branch), and only then does the remote DWIM run. The
`refExists` helper is shared by the branch and tag probes.

Covered by a real-repository test (slashed tag detaches HEAD at the tag commit,
creates neither a `v1.0` nor a `tags/v1.0` branch, and wins over a same-named
local branch), mocked router-level tests for all three resolution paths, and the
sidebar integration test now checks out `release/v1.0`.
